# PlateForge research handoff

For source reconstruction, font matching, die-era grouping and reference-gallery imports, read [the reconstruction skill](skills/plate-reference-reconstruction/SKILL.md) and [the project workflow](docs/plate-reconstruction-workflow.md).

Keep source evidence separate from model coverage. A year on a renewal decal is not a manufacture date; similar lettering across classes is not proof of identical tooling. Preserve uncertain and undated evidence explicitly.

Existing research ledgers live under `docs/research/`. Register national Canadian plates under `ca-federal`; keep provincial National Defence N plates in their province. Reuse the data-driven kit with `jurisdiction: 'CA'`, rather than inheriting B.C. manufacturer claims.

Use the user's current authorization and normal project checks. This handoff does not introduce an additional approval step.
