# 1987 Royal motorcade lettering

The serial remains an approximate smooth paint-body reconstruction. This revision replaces all observed R/O/Y/A/L/1/8 with explicit curved contours and keeps one profile for ROYAL1–ROYAL20. R/O/Y/A/L/1 are independently compared in ROYAL1 and ROYAL18; 8 appears in ROYAL18 only. Digits 0, 2–7 and 9 retain coherent constructed fallbacks with unconfirmed physical die identity.

The source article attributes production to Astrographic and identifies the Ham Radio blank. This does not independently identify every glyph's tooling. The source photographs have wear, paint spread, deformation and raised shoulders; those are retained in photographic crops but excluded from intended smooth construction.

The revised observations are narrow rounded-rectangular O; broad flat A top, rounded triangular counter and a large arched lower opening; curved Y junction; raised L toe; flagged 1; and a pinched but continuous 8 waist. Approximate paint-body widths are R .47, O .45–.46, Y .46–.47, A .51, L .45–.47, 1 .27–.28 and 8 .48 of height. Typical intended body weight is about 9–12 cap units, lighter than the previous generic 11.5-unit skeleton in the bowls.

The proposed serial placement is center 153 mm, baseline 109.5 mm, cap height 66.5 mm and 12-unit tracking. Full-plate overlays use estimated outer edges mapped to the 300 × 150 mm plate plane. This corrects coarse perspective only, not lens distortion or the bend visible in ROYAL1. Shape comparisons independently match photographed crop height and center; widths are not fitted separately. Approximate threshold mask overlap is included as a diagnostic, not an accuracy certificate.
