# B.C. event artwork and lettering updates

The Centennial preset uses the user-supplied geometric artwork, including both outlined wordmarks, joined 100, mountains, skyline and water. Green 1886 and blue 1986 columns now use the uploaded Frankfurter Std Regular digit outlines and align with the photographed columns. Complete artwork and glyphs scale uniformly. No font software is bundled.

The retail EXPO86 souvenir, prototype and two booster variants use the tested Frankfurter Std Medium outlines. The six native outlines preserve counters, curves and overshoots. Pair gaps are calibrated against the whole retail photograph with one shared cap height and baseline, without fitting individual glyph widths. The full-run overlay is available on the [interactive comparison](https://projects.ahmadjalil.com/plateforge/bc-font-comparisons/expo86/). The other Expo serials and event symbols retain separate constructions.

The [six-character photographic test](font-comparison.md) compares the uploaded Regular, official Medium specimen and width-only Regular variants. Medium won all six comparisons and 54 paint-threshold trials. The production vectors overlap independent Medium raster specimens by 97.6–99.5% after equal-height registration. Whole-run placement calibration is recorded in expo-spacing-calibration.json. These checks verify visual matching and conversion, not historical font attribution.

The Expo NWT pavilion plate shares the NWT reconstruction used by the Explore Canada's Arctic base: die-cut silhouette, independent inset border and four slotted mounts. The body mask clips every layer and preserves transparent exterior, foot gaps and holes in preview and export. The lettering layout is checked against EXPO86 and EXPO8 photographs. Shared reconstruction does not certify identical tooling or measured historical dimensions.

The 1951 Royal Visit plate embeds the exact uploaded isolated Canadian coat-of-arms PNG, fitted uniformly. Downloaded SVG embeds the original image bytes; PNG exports retain the supplied appearance. Position and size remain approximate. The 1987 Royal plate uses one coherent Astrographic-style construction checked against ROYAL1 and ROYAL18, rather than mixing individual photograph traces with fallback glyphs. Other digits in the documented 1–20 range remain constructions, not independently verified outlines.

Both the 1997 APEC motorcade globe and military sticker use the supplied APEC vector kit. Geometry, outlined APEC lettering, flat colours and clipping are preserved; IDs are namespaced. The kit combines APEC brochure vectors with a reconstructed historical halo and is not a verified 1997 printing master. See apec-provenance.json and coat-of-arms-provenance.json for source hashes and placement limits.

Monochrome artwork now inherits the selected plate ink; this corrects the 1952 National Defence totem while retaining explicit artwork colour overrides. PCMR now uses smooth filled reconstructions of the observed P.C.M.R. letters and a separate compact C/O/7/1 column. This is an individual hand-painted Company 71 design, not a province-wide stamping alphabet. The [photographic comparison](https://projects.ahmadjalil.com/plateforge/bc-font-comparisons/pcmr/) exposes the original, current render and approximate full-frame lettering overlay; detailed limits are in pcmr-company-71.md.

Validation covers production previews, mobile layouts and standalone SVG/PNG exports for affected presets, including exact coat-of-arms image bytes and transparent NWT body boundaries. Full test and deployment results are reported with the update. Paint colours, embossing, wear and historical tooling identity remain approximate.

The two corner marks on the APEC motorcade plate now use the supplied emblem-only Asia Pacific crane/waves and full Pacific Gateway panel vectors, preserving source geometry, palette, fish components and transparent gaps. The APEC description credits Amy Ho and links the 1997 Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada publication for the designer, symbolism and contemporary trade context. The 1994 Royal Visit and prototype share the supplied crowned EIIR maple badge, including its outlined cypher and circular clipping.

The current NWT Spectacular preset uses the exact uploaded Aurora Over Arctic Wilderness PNG beneath the existing lettering, inset border and body mask. The image fills the plane uniformly with centre cropping; the bear cut shape and four round mounting holes remain transparent. The older Explore 1986 preset retains its white background. See supplied-event-emblems-provenance.json for attachment filenames, hashes and construction limits. The supplied artwork is a rendition for PlateForge, not a recovered printing master.
