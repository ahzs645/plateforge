# EXPO86 font comparison · 2026-10-05

Frankfurter Std Medium is the strongest tested visual match across all six photographed characters. The supplied Regular was rendered directly from its OTF and compared with the official Medium live preview customized to EXPO86. No Medium font file is bundled or installed in PlateForge. The existing souvenir font remains unchanged; this update publishes the evidence and interactive raster-specimen comparison.

Each source-paint box was selected independently from the original 400×207 photograph. E and X are isolated; P/O and 8/6 have joined edges. The O box includes its complete outer bowl; the 6 box excludes the adjacent 8 body. Source boxes, paint threshold, retained counters and limitations are recorded in font-comparison.json. This near-frontal scan is not an exact perspective registration. Glyph ink height and source-box centre were matched, retaining native candidate width. Width-only sweeps range from 80% to 120%, separately per character, with padded scoring canvases retaining all candidate ink outside the source box.

Medium wins both direct comparisons and comparisons against the best width-only Regular variant for E, X, P, O, 8 and 6. Opening width normalized by photographed character height is especially diagnostic: O is approximately 0.56 in the photograph and Medium, versus 0.37 in Regular. The photograph's 8 openings are approximately 0.15 / 0.25 of height; Medium reproduces the small-upper/large-lower balance, while Regular has two tiny openings. These are raster comparisons, not font-identification probabilities or evidence of identical physical printing tooling.

The public comparison uses the original externally hosted photograph with actual photographic crops, transparent candidate specimen rasters, opacity and width controls, and per-character findings. It contains no source outlines traced from the plate and no Medium font file. Exact original typography attribution remains unconfirmed.

Sources: [BCpl8s original](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/Expo-86/Expo86-Souvenir(mid).jpg), [BCpl8s article](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/Expo-86.htm), [official MyFonts family](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/frankfurter-font-itc). Official Medium preview identifier e094dede1f515189f4972f03427ad6eb; browser specimen size 192px. ITC font attribution is retained.

Sensitivity: Medium won all 54 direct comparison trials across six glyphs and nine dark-blue paint thresholds. Standard ligature and GPOS inspection found no shaping adjustment affecting EXPO86 in the supplied Regular, ruling out an omitted shaping feature as the stroke/counter cause. Browser checks passed all six original-photo overlays, candidate switching, opacity/width/reset controls, full-photo expansion and mobile layout, with no page or failed-resource errors. The production build passed.
