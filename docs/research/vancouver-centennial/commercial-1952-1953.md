# Commercial 1952 base and 1953 renewal

The [BCpl8s commercial 1952–1954 study](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/CommercialTruck1952-1954.html) documents the 335 × 137 mm aluminium commercial base. Its long serial occupies most of the upper left; the date and thunderbird stand at right, and the complete BRITISH COLUMBIA legend runs below. The passenger base's maple leaf was omitted. The existing detailed thunderbird vector is reused without the leaf.

Reference photographs checked:

- [1952 C29·419, full base](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/Commercial/1952/1952-C29419(XL).jpg), 2000 × 819 pixels: serial height/position, large 52 date, lower legend, separate fixing holes and emblem position.
- [1953 tab 443161](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/Commercial/1953/1953-443161(XL).jpg), 723 × 1500 pixels: commercial tab silhouette, rounded right corners, top/right/bottom rim, two holes, 53 pair, thunderbird and lower-left notch.
- [1953 tab 459961](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/Commercial/1953/1953-459961(XL).jpg), 328 × 720 pixels: independent shape/year cross-check.
- [Commercial tab mounted with straps](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/Commercial/1953/Tab(Commercial).jpg), 132 × 300 pixels: actual piece and fastening method.
- [New-registration C97·537](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/Commercial/1953/1952-C97537(XL).jpg), 1200 × 491 pixels: the blank date panel on new 1953 bases. This differs from renewing an existing 1952 plate.

The renderer now retains the complete 1952 plate under a separately drawn commercial tab. The tab extends over the full plate height, and its lower-left clearance notch exposes the final A. The previous 43.55 × 123.3 mm rectangular panel omitted the notch and removed the base year; it did not represent a separate renewal piece properly.

The tab body is estimated as 63 × 140 mm from the body bounds in multiple photographs, with the right edge aligned to the 335 mm base and 1.5 mm extending above and below. Commercial dimensions are not stated in the source; this is an estimate. It should not inherit the wider 90 × 140 mm passenger tab. The 53 pair uses the existing renewal-tab year reconstruction; it is not the base serial alphabet.

The source's paragraph says the commercial tab "does not include the thunderbord totem", but all linked commercial-tab photographs clearly contain the thunderbird and omit the maple leaf. The implementation follows the visible specimens. This discrepancy is recorded rather than treating the sentence as reliable visual geometry.

Tab serials were independently stamped in the 400,001–485,000 range. They are not the registration number; an invented repeated base serial is therefore omitted. Aged paint, precise physical dimensions, embossing shoulders, fastening straps and every glyph outline remain approximate. The serial retains the shared rounded 1940–54 digit masters with a heavier C, the short rectangular separator seen in C29·419, and observed pair spacing. A separate broad rounded legend construction replaces the overly condensed province line; its 20 mm cap and wide final A were checked against independent paint boxes. The heavy 52 date has its own constant-stroke construction. No new manufactured alphabet is asserted.

The deployable comparison at `/bc-font-comparisons/commercial1952/` retains the complete rectified photograph and 24 original photographic occurrences (seven serial marks, two date digits and fifteen province letters). All overlays keep plate placement and scale; duplicate 9s, repeated Is, and serial/date 2s are shown separately. Residual contour/height differences remain visible rather than marked as exact matches. Source paint boxes and current geometric bounds are recorded alongside the report.
