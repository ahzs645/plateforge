# Lieutenant Governor and royal crown artwork

The Lieutenant Governor crest uses the SVG from the user’s `Archive.zip`. The flag’s rectangular background is omitted; the shield, crown and ten-leaf circlet retain their supplied outlines and red, white and blue details. Yellow `#f7e017` becomes gold `#c5a34a`. A royal-blue circular plaque with a gold rim fills the black plate’s height. Mirrored SVG `use` references are expanded so exported and gallery instances do not share conflicting IDs.

The royal crown plate uses the supplied `Edward-crown-goldenrod.svg`, preserving its geometry and goldenrod colour. Both original vectors are retained in `src/templates/bc/assets/`; their inert paths and presentation attributes are imported by `scripts/import-governor-artwork.py`.

The older Lieutenant Governor coat of arms is a **filled gold relief reconstruction**, not a copy of an official die-struck master. A separate solid-body outer contour and subdued detail layer replace the fine line arms previously borrowed from the historical tin plates. Those historical masters are unchanged. Exact contour and metal relief remain approximate.

[BCpl8s’s Lieutenant Governor study](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/LGpl8.htm) identifies Pressed Metal Products Ltd. of Vancouver as the emblem maker. It describes the crest’s introduction in 2008, but also shows the older arms [in 2003](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/LiuetenantGovenor/LG(2003).jpg) and [in 2016](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/LiuetenantGovenor/LG(2016).jpg). The older design’s catalogue coverage therefore extends to the 2016 photograph and overlaps the crest. Its precise introduction remains unknown; the existing 1998 display anchor is approximate.

The crest’s source reference is [the full plaque photograph](https://www.bcpl8s.ca/images/LiuetenantGovenor/LG(XL).jpg). The new circular diameter reaches both plate edges; assumed plate dimensions, paint colours, enamel finish and plaque fitting remain approximate. This update does not depict the removable chrome plate holder.
