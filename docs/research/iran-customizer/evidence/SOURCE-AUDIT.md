# Iran plate inventory: bounded evidence freeze

Research freeze: 2 October 2026. The inventory covers every distinct plate specimen or layout pictured on the two requested Iran pages, not every issue ever produced in Iran.

- `inventory-rows.json`: 55 flat rows, stable IDs, source/image URLs, local reference files, transcriptions, geometry, dates, confidence and limitations
- `inventory.json`: same rows plus 11 recovered earlier observations, chronology resolution, access barriers and scope
- `date-resolution.json`: date ledger
- `special-classes-contact.png`, `freezones-contact.png`, `recovered-contact.png`: inspected contact sheets
- Six original WLP sheets and individual study crops; 27 Wikipedia class/free-zone diagram downloads

## Scope and exclusions

52 requested-source rows comprise 23 WLP specimens (one explicitly fake), 20 Wikipedia class/previous-format illustrations, seven local free-zone illustrations, and two real vehicle photographs. Three extra rows document the 2017 unified free-zone private/public/government reform from contemporary reporting, with geometry explicitly unverified. The 11 recovered DNA references are separate supporting material, never passed off as images from the two requested pages.

The WLP June 2026 sheet explicitly labels its green police-looking example **Fake!** That row is excluded from authentic reconstruction. Wikipedia illustrations are drawn examples, not authenticated manufactured plates. The archives' source photos have no established redistribution permission. Public outputs should use original study artwork and link the reference sources.

## Findings that must affect the editor

1. Keep city-initial/year-tab, full-city side panel, two-row city/extension, city-band, national long, international-travel and free-zone layouts separate. A modern historical-vehicle class is not a historical-era registration.
2. No source here proves a countrywide 1979 layout change. Foreign-travel TEH and THR plates are additional plates on a parallel track.
3. Current diplomatic/service right-box headings are **سیاسی / سرویس**, not ایران. Modern temporary plates add a separate expiry compartment. Previous diplomatic/service and temporary layouts are compact and structurally distinct.
4. The seven local free-zone drawings are not interchangeable badges. Chabahar has white lower numerals on blue. Qeshm has a white badge column. Their source drawings contain mismatched Persian/Latin sample numbers; the editor should synchronize repeat values by default.
5. The actual WLP 1964 side-panel extension appears **13?** in enlarged pixels; earlier recovered notes said 12. Preserve the uncertain small transcription. The identical 41629 Tehran specimen is placed under passenger on WLP but attributed to diplomatic in recovered DNA notes. Keep the class conflict explicit.

## Date resolution

- SH1326 = 1947/48 is the earliest recovered dated specimen, not a first-issue claim. WLP's pictured passenger timeline starts at 1949
- UNIDO's automotive study, section 2.1, printed p.22, says passenger registration changed from four to five figures in SH1348 = 1969/70. Search-index text verified; PDF pixels unavailable. Earlier five-digit collector specimens mean this cannot become a blanket four-digit validation rule
- WLP labels city-band plates 1993; recovered sources include a 1998 attribution. This remains a collector-date dispute
- WLP labels the national family 2003. A contemporary 26 April 2004 report explicitly says rollout began the preceding Esfand, SH1382 = February–March 2004, and already states 52×11 cm. Wikipedia's 2005 dimensional statement is not a defensible universal start
- Diplomatic/service format was unveiled 16 Esfand 1394 = 6 March 2016; operation was planned for Ordibehesht 1395 = April–May 2016. Unveiling is not completed replacement
- Unified free-zone pilot: 10 Mordad 1396 = 1 August 2017, Qeshm. Report supports 360×150 mm and three class backgrounds. Its downloaded news image is a generic placeholder, so no exact 2017 geometry is authenticated
