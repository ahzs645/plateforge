4. Colour, substrate edges and font identity

The visible silhouettes support rounded 2004–2009 substrates and square 2010–2016 substrates. The renderer now respects the audited corner-radius setting in its earlier solid branches. The chosen clean radii remain approximations: 0.08 times height for 2004 and 0.10 for 2005–2009. Damaged 2006 and tightly cropped 2008 edges have lower confidence.

The January–June 2017 square layout and July-onward rounded layout are retained. ICBC's Bulletin 14, dated 3 February 2016, describes rounded corners, a slight colour change and reduced font size beginning with July 2017 expiry decals. It provides no measured radius, cutting tolerance, colour standard or decal typeface name.

The material report shows explicitly located substrate colour patches, their decoded RGB medians and variation. The existing palette is retained because no calibrated target or controlled capture conditions accompany the photographs. A digital mismatch is visible; an exact physical replacement colour is not established. ICBC's Bulletin 17 also documents fading affecting dates, controls and barcodes, so pale or broken old ink is not automatically a printing-master feature.

Useful next evidence is specific: an intact measured sample or cutting drawing for dimensions/radii; controlled colour measurements or production colour specifications; multiple lighting angles for surface/security-layer behaviour; and original artwork/font records for a historical typeface identification.

Walk through the font, spacing, width and placement method

How this overlay pass separates font, spacing, width and placement

This review starts from the complete first-pass commit 24689246670c25f91e0d1676a0647f053dbca68e. Every comparison has an exact catalogue variant, month and control string. A control is transcribed from the photograph; the filename is only an identifier. Leading zeroes remain text. Supplemental image captions and preliminary transcriptions that proved wrong are retained in the evidence correction logs.

1. Establish what the source can support

Keep the original image beside each cropped or thresholded derivative. Record its URL, credit, licence, original dimensions and SHA-256. Two crops of the same marked specimen are one specimen, not independent corroboration. A government illustration is labelled as an illustration. A sample with an all-zero control can support its visible layout without establishing normal issuance.

For shape comparisons, reject empty masks, clipped ink, inadequate contrast, insufficient painted height and an unexpected number of characters. Vary the threshold and validate every sample. Rejected samples retain their reason and cannot enter an accepted range. A whole run may have stable extents even when blur joins its characters; in that case, withhold individual-glyph scores.

Tiny province lettering can sometimes support approximate whole-line location and extent. Five to eight source pixels do not support a detailed capital reconstruction. The larger independent photographs supply that evidence.

2. Use two different overlays for two different questions

Shape overlay: isolate a word, number or character and give source and renderer the same actual painted height. Scale each uniformly, preserving its width-to-height ratio, then align horizontally on its centre. Magenta shows source-only ink, blue model-only ink, and dark their overlap. This reveals counter shape, stroke weight, terminal shape and disproportionate letters.

The width ratio is:

(model painted width / model painted height) / (source painted width / source painted height)

A value of 1.075 means the model is about 7.5% broad at equal painted height. It does not mean that the model is 92.5% accurate. A ratio of one can still hide the wrong J hook, an A counter, a narrow first letter and a wide last letter.

Composition overlay: give the complete decal and photograph the same frame height and align their horizontal centres. Preserve both original aspect ratios. This exposes baselines, side margins, line spacing, province position, control boxes, borders and barcodes. A broad photographic frame remains broad; the renderer is not stretched to make its edges coincide.

Neither overlay rectifies photographic perspective. Tilt, blur, damage and ink spread remain explicit limits. These overlays are diagnostics, not physical measurements of a decal or authentication scores.

An existing native font is useful when its diagnostic forms are closer. A matching overall width alone is insufficient. New vector profiles explicitly refuse fallback characters and contain only the observed letters or numerals. Their labels describe reconstructions, not confirmed historical font names.

4. Choose the narrowest supported scope

The fixed 04 can be shared across 2004 because six month photographs support the same year construction. A margin calibrated from one JUN, MAY or JUL specimen is an exact-month adjustment. That scope is a software calibration choice, not a historical claim that a printing design changed that month.

The 1998 Type II and thick-border 1999 MAY masters remain separate. Early and later modern numeral groups remain separate. Modern year and control digits also remain separate. Fixed province words do not become evidence for an entire unobserved alphabet.

5. Verify the assembled result

Render the actual production engine after integration. Inspect each changed reference against the clean baseline, then every selectable month in each affected family. Verify actual painted bounds, collisions, the printed glyph sequence, exact controls, control-panel containment and natural fitting where required. Check glyph components and counters from rasterized vector paths.

The application preflight uses its offered format/decal combinations, validation and actual glyph output, including a leading-zero control. The full application tests and TypeScript/production build check integration. Browser interaction and exported PNG/PDF parity are a separate gate; an Inkscape render or a passing server-side selection is not a browser pass.

The final ledger retains changed, corroborated and unresolved cases together. It records what improved, the remaining discrepancy, the relevant source and the next kind of evidence that would resolve it. Calibrated colour, precise cutting dimensions and historical typeface identity require evidence beyond these uncalibrated photographs.
