# Reconstruct plates from references

Use for plate research, lettering corrections, die-era grouping, and importing a photographic gallery. Read the project-specific details in `../../docs/plate-reconstruction-workflow.md`.

1. Inventory every photograph and every plate within composite images. Record the source URL, caption, observed serial, role, palette, date evidence, and intended preset. Account for duplicates and exclusions. An inspected gallery is not proof of exhaustive historical coverage.
2. Inspect the originals. Separate jurisdiction, use, shell size, main serial, smaller stamped legends, screened lettering and decals. A plate's year label, decal year, base introduction, manufacture date and a website update date are different evidence.
3. Group by observed lettering and source attribution. Compare multiple examples, including passenger and other classes. Preserve overlapping suppliers, small-format alphabets and uncertain assignments. Do not infer a manufacturer's identity from similar letters alone.
4. Identify physical plate corners before perspective correction. Keep original and rectified photographs accessible. Withhold precise contour claims when perspective, wear, blur or insufficient resolution prevents registration.
5. Isolate each observed character occurrence, including repetitions in one serial. Keep its source plate/run/index. Show the photographic crop; a trace is a separate derived artifact.
6. Compare both placement and shape. Placement retains the plate-plane position. Shape comparison matches center and height uniformly; do not independently stretch width to conceal a mismatch. Inspect counters, waist, stroke weight, terminals and slant as well as the bounding box.
7. Reconstruct a reusable master only where evidence supports it. Separate lettering from palette and layout. For fixed words, outlined glyphs from an identified supplied font give portable exports; preserve native advances and disclose any whole-word fitting. Unobserved glyphs remain constructed or unsupported.
8. Label evidence locally: photographed, source-attributed, visually supported, reconstructed, uncertain, or undated. Do not certify all glyphs or identical physical tooling from a family assignment. Date spans from galleries remain source-caption spans.
9. Verify actual rendering beside the photograph, then exercise generated/typed serials, palette changes, SVG/PNG export, mobile layout and metadata. A successful build does not establish a visual match.
10. Save the ledger, sources, limitations, scripts and validation results in the repository. Update comparison previews when geometry changes. Complete authorized publication and check the live site before claiming deployment.

When using parallel agents, divide ownership by files/families and consolidate one coverage ledger. Delegation does not replace inspection or final verification. Follow the session's delegation authorization.
