BC early trailer stamps

The 1925 plate is unprefixed. From 1927, the T is a separate smaller stamp; the 1948 TR run retains reduced letters. The same upright rounded BC construction is used across these early trailer reconstructions. It is separate from the small passenger province legend.

1921–22 retains its interlaced monogram. The narrow 1927–30 base and approximately 2:1 1931–48 bodies are separate presets. Shared visible forms support comparison, but identical physical tooling and exact numerical contours remain unconfirmed. Colours are approximate readings from aged plates.
