EXPO 86: which Frankfurter cut matches?

Frankfurter Std Medium is the strongest tested visual match.

All six characters were compared with the original photograph. Medium improves the strokes and counters across E, X, P, O, 8 and 6. A width adjustment to the supplied Regular does not correct its much smaller openings.

The live souvenir now uses the tested Medium outlines. “Current PlateForge outlines” overlays the actual six vectors used in the preview and SVG/PNG exports. No font file is bundled. This visual comparison does not confirm the original manufacturer's typography specification.
