B.C. municipal lettering and centennial bases

Vancouver's screened Vehicle for Hire, TAXI CAB and VANCOUVER words now use outlined Frankfurter Std Medium—the digital font family used for the EXPO86 souvenir. Their embossed serials remain a separate municipal reconstruction. Font-family similarity does not identify the historical manufacturer or prove identical physical tooling.

Green bands are photographed on the blank 1986 base,1995 for-hire and1996 taxi specimens; a blue band is photographed on the1997 taxi. These are explicit colour alternatives, not annual production rules. The Vancouver 100 emblem stays underneath the renewal decal; the new blank preset exposes it.

Prince George1959 has broad upper lettering, tall lower VEHICLE and rounded serif numbers/date. Two clear specimens 15 and 000 support that comparison. The1962 plate has a separate layout. Its broken corner is damage, which is not reproduced. Unseen digits and exact outlines remain approximate.

The three Prince George bicycle examples are checked and shown separately. Their original renderer remains approximate; these oval tags do not establish shared tooling with the vehicle plates. The source attributes George Hewitt manufacture with estimate caveats.
